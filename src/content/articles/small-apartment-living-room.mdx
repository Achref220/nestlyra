Small rooms expand when you stop fighting the footprint and start editing the silhouette.

## Choose legs over bulk

Furniture that shows floor underneath feels lighter. A sofa on tapered legs and a table with open space underneath buy visual square footage.

## Hang curtains high

Mount rods close to the ceiling and let fabric puddle or kiss the floor. Vertical lines lift the room’s proportions instantly.

## Use one large art piece

A gallery wall can shrink a small room. One generous frame or textile gives the eye a resting place and reads more intentional.

For collected depth once the layout is clear, see [layered living rooms that feel collected](/articles/layered-living-rooms/).

## Multipurpose without looking clever

Ottomans with storage, nesting tables, and wall-mounted lights earn their place quietly. Avoid gadgets that announce themselves as “space-saving.”

## Mirror light, not clutter

Place a mirror where it reflects a window or lamp — never a messy corner.
